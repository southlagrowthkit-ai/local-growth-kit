Founding terms

Pay only for accepted, qualified requests.

The first accepted qualified lead is $1. The next five accepted qualified leads are $20 each. The pilot is limited to a maximum of six accepted qualified leads and maximum $101 total.

01

Small and cancellable

No setup fee, no monthly fee, no contract, and no automatic renewal. Either party may stop before another lead is generated.

02

Exclusive by design

Each qualified request goes to one provider only during review. Consumer information is never silently distributed to multiple businesses.

03

A written decision

You have 48 hours after delivery to accept or reject in writing. Unresolved requests are not invoiced unless a different rule was agreed beforehand.

What counts

Clear qualification. Fair rejection.

Qualified

Intent, valid contact details, job details, timeframe, an agreed service-area match, permission to share, and no prior delivery.

Valid rejection

Invalid contact, duplicate, outside the agreed service area, unsupported service, missing consent, or a demonstrably fake submission.

Not a rejection reason

A genuine requester who does not answer immediately, book, accept the price, or proceed is not automatically invalid.

A lead is an inquiry, not a sale. There is no booking or revenue guarantee.
