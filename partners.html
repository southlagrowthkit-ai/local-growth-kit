What makes a lead qualified?

A request must pass every rule below before it can be charged. The goal is a transparent, measurable pilot—not vague exposure.

01

Real request

The consumer intentionally requests mobile-detailing information and supplies a valid contact method.

02

Inside your scope

The location and requested service match the area and services agreed before launch.

03

Clear consent

The consumer permits sharing the request with one matched detailer and being contacted about it.

04

Useful details

Vehicle type, requested service, ZIP or service location, and desired timeframe are included.

05

Exclusive routing

The request is delivered to one participating provider during the acceptance window.

06

Not a duplicate

The same consumer request has not already been delivered to your business.

Accept or reject within 48 hours.

A rejected lead needs one clear, permitted reason.

  • Invalid contact information
  • Duplicate request
  • Outside agreed service area
  • Unsupported service
  • No valid sharing consent
  • Demonstrably fake submission

Not automatic rejection reasons

A genuine lead does not become invalid merely because the person does not answer immediately, decides not to book, or objects to the provider’s price.

No inflated promises

South LA Growth Kit does not guarantee bookings, revenue, conversion rate, or profitability. The test measures whether we can deliver genuine, consented requests that match the written rules.
